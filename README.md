# FIRST LEGO League 2026-27 

This repository is the team's workspace for the **FIRST LEGO League 2026-27 season**.

The purpose of this repository is to keep our work organized and, more importantly, document our journey throughout the season.

We will use it to capture:

- Innovation Project research and development
- Robot Game strategy
- Robot design and programming
- Testing and improvements
- Core Values activities
- Team meetings and decisions
- Competition preparation
- Photos, videos, and supporting materials

## 01-problem-discovery

This is where we identify and understand possible biodiversity problems.

### ideas/

Put **new problem ideas** here.

One file can be created for each promising idea.

Examples:

```text
storm-drain-invasive-seeds.md
bird-feeder-disease.md
wildlife-fence-barrier.md
```

At this stage, don't worry about designing the solution.

First understand:

- What is the problem?
- Who or what is affected?
- How does it affect biodiversity?
- Where does it happen?
- Why does it happen?
- How significant is the problem?

### research/

Put research supporting the problems here.

Examples:

- Scientific articles
- Government information
- Biodiversity studies
- Statistics
- Research notes
- Website links
- Papers
- Videos
- Notes from books

Always record the source of important information.

### problem-selection/

Use this folder when deciding **which problem the team will pursue**.

Examples:

```text
problem-comparison.md
selection-criteria.md
final-problem.md
```

Document why ideas were accepted or rejected.

# 04 - Existing Solutions

```text
01-innovation-project/04-existing-solutions/
```

Before creating our solution, we need to understand **what already exists**.

Research:

- Existing products
- Existing inventions
- Research projects
- Patents
- Apps
- Government solutions
- Commercial solutions

For each solution ask:

1. How does it work?
2. What does it do well?
3. What does it NOT solve?
4. Is it expensive?
5. Is it difficult to use?
6. Does it create another problem?
7. What could be improved?

The goal is to identify the **gap**.

```text
Existing Solution
       ↓
What works?
       ↓
What doesn't work?
       ↓
GAP
       ↓
Our opportunity
```

# How We Will Work

Throughout the season we want to follow this approach:

```text
ASK QUESTIONS
      ↓
RESEARCH
      ↓
OBSERVE
      ↓
TALK TO EXPERTS
      ↓
UNDERSTAND THE PROBLEM
      ↓
STUDY EXISTING SOLUTIONS
      ↓
IDENTIFY THE GAP
      ↓
BRAINSTORM
      ↓
DESIGN
      ↓
BUILD
      ↓
TEST
      ↓
LEARN
      ↓
IMPROVE
      ↓
SHARE
```

The goal of this repository is not to make everything look perfect.

**Failures, experiments, rejected ideas, design changes, and lessons learned are part of our FLL journey and should be documented.**
